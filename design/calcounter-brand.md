# CalCounter identity
Generated with the built-in image generation tool.
Prompt: Create a premium minimalist iPhone app icon for CalCounter, a fast calorie and macro food logger. One iconic interlocking double-C monogram, formed from two bold clean rounded geometric bands, subtly suggesting a plate and a count ring. Electric fresh lime green and warm ivory on an opaque deep forest charcoal background. Sophisticated restrained dimensional finish, crisp silhouette legible at 32 pixels, centered, generous safe margins. Full-bleed square 1024x1024 canvas, no rounded outer corners (iOS applies its own mask), no border, no surrounding mockup, no lettering or words, no numbers, no utensils, no flame, no extra symbols. Finished production logo/icon, striking and polished.
Assets: public/calcounter-icon-{1024,512,192,180}.png. The native AppIcon uses the same 1024 px master. Existing bundle ID, storage keys, server URLs and sync identifiers are retained to preserve installed data and connections.
